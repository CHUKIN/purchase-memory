# Results: carousel summary, discount in card, category tiles

Level: Обычный. Frontend only (no backend, no migrations). Built on top of the
uncommitted "Back goes one step" stage (РЕЗУЛЬТАТЫ_НАЗАД_ПО_ШАГАМ.md).

## Done

- **Steps = products.** `line-review/carouselSteps.ts`: a product is a card;
  discounts linked to it (`discountLinks`) are shown inside that card. A
  discount on no product (receipt-level, or before any product) keeps a card
  of its own («Скидка на весь чек» / «Скидка»). The counter reads «Товар N из M»;
  dots are per card.
- **Discount row in the card:** «Скидка −7,86 · к оплате 36,70» + «Убрать».
  Tapping the row opens the name and «Размер скидки» fields (opens by itself
  when the amount is missing; the product's dot is then orange).
  - Receipt draft: «Убрать» removes at once, «Вернуть» while the card is shown.
  - Saved purchase: «Убрать» asks first («Убрать скидку?» / «Убрать»; removal is saved to the server).
  - While a field of the card is focused, the card's grouping is frozen, so a
    discount briefly larger than its product does not jump out of the card.
- «Верно, дальше» is the filled primary button and marks the product together
  with its card's discounts (draft and saved purchase), also when only the
  discount was corrected (`onSaveLines(changes, alsoApprove)`).
- **Draft summary step** (`receipts/CaptureSummary.tsx`): «Всё проверено»
  (or «Итог покупки» + «Проверено товаров: X из N» when not all checked),
  store · date, Товаров / Скидки / По строкам, warning box «Итог на чеке …»
  with the "price already includes the discount" hint and «Взять сумму строк»,
  or «Сходится с итогом на чеке»; «Вернуться к товарам». The footer holds the
  big «Сохранить покупку» (same mutation, draftWarnings / «Всё равно
  сохранить» unchanged). While checking products the footer has
  «Магазин, дата и итог» and «Сохранить сейчас».
- Last product: «Верно, к итогу»; a checked card in the draft shows a small ✓
  (unmark) + «Дальше» / «К итогу».
- The summary is a history layer (`useHistoryLayer`): back / chevron return
  to the product it was opened from; saving from it replaces its entry.
- Saved purchase review: A applied; it keeps «Верно» on the last card (no
  summary). Corrections are now kept per line (product + its discount) and
  saved in one request (`onSaveLines`).
- **Products list:** 40 px category tile (lucide icon + tint per seeded group,
  found by `systemKey` or by the fixed seed id, so renamed groups keep it;
  household groups → neutral tag; no category → grey tag). Subtitle: leaf
  category, ✦ for suggested (`role=img`, «предложено»), brand, purchases;
  «Проверить» in warning colour; «без категории». A product photo, if any,
  replaces the tile. Data comes from the existing categories endpoint.
- i18n ru/en, help (review-save, receipt-lines-photo, products), stories
  (CaptureLineReview: DiscountsInCards, SummaryMismatch, SummaryMatches;
  LineCarousel: Discount, LastProductToSummary, ReceiptDiscount;
  Catalog: CategoryTilesMobileRu/En).

## Limitations

- The app is light only (`app/ionic.ts`), so tiles have no dark variants.
- Deleting a product («Удалить») still deletes only that line; its discount
  then attaches to the previous product (unchanged behaviour).
- Undo («Вернуть») only in the draft; in a saved purchase removal is confirmed.
  The undo is dropped when another line is deleted.
- The grouping freeze lasts only while a field has focus; after leaving the
  field a discount larger than its product moves to its own receipt-discount card.
- In the saved-purchase review «Выбрать товар для строки N» uses the product
  number («Товар N»), not the line number.
- CatalogSearchModal rows were not changed (no category chip there).
