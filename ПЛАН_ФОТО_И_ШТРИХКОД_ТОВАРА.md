# Plan: product photo + barcode, find in store, photo recognition

Owner asked 2026-10-16. Status: PLAN ONLY, no stage approved. Each stage needs
the owner's explicit yes; deploy needs a separate yes.

## Goal

Remember a product by its look and barcode, then find it in a shop in seconds:
was it bought, when, where, price, own rating. Later: find by photo without a
barcode, and attach photos to many products from one photo of the haul.

Already required in `ПРОДУКТОВАЯ_СЕССИЯ.md` (package photos and barcodes belong
to the product card; several barcodes/photos per product; barcode is the
primary identity of "this product"). Not implemented today: `Product` has no
barcode and no photo.

## Facts and constraints

- Polish receipts have no EAN per line, so the first barcode ↔ product link is
  made by a person once; later receipts reach the product through aliases.
- iOS Safari has no native `BarcodeDetector`; scanning needs a JS/WASM decoder
  over the camera (candidate: `barcode-detector` polyfill on `zxing-wasm`;
  verify version and iPhone PWA behaviour at stage start).
- Storage: product photos go through a product image store contract next to
  `IReceiptImageStore` (local folder / Supabase Storage). Compressed copy only
  (~150–250 KB). Supabase free = 1 GB storage — track usage.
- Open Food Facts: free, no key, ODbL; called from the backend with only the
  barcode; coverage of Polish products is partial — prefill only, never trusted.
- Render free cold start (~1 min) hurts the "in the shop" scenario; ties to the
  infrastructure decision in `ЧТО_ДАЛЬШЕ.md` §3.
- Variant model (`product → variant → package edition`) stays later; barcodes
  attach to `Product` now and can move to variants later without data loss.
- Every stage updates help (`helpText.ts`, `topics.ts`, `HelpLink`).

## Stages

### Stage 1 — Prototype and screens (no prod changes) — DONE 2026-10-16, see `РЕЗУЛЬТАТЫ_ПРОТОТИП_СКАНЕРА.md`
- Storybook mock of: product card with photos + barcodes, «Сканировать» on
  Главная, scan result (found / not found / pick a product), "attach to line".
- Throwaway scanner page in dev: check decoding speed and reliability on the
  owner's iPhone (installed PWA, camera permission, poor light, curved packs).
- Open Food Facts hit rate on ~20 barcodes the owner scans at home.
- Output: owner picks the screens; library chosen with numbers.
- Model: Sonnet implementer, Sonnet review. Cost: free.

### Stage 2 — Backend: barcodes and photos of a product
- Domain: `ProductBarcode` (EAN-8/13, UPC; unique across products; source:
  scan / manual / OFF), `ProductPhoto` (storage key, created by, created at,
  main flag). Several of each per product. Audit trail entries.
- Migration SQLite + Postgres (check on a throwaway Postgres, never the owner
  DB). Product image store contract + local and Supabase implementations.
- API: add/remove barcode, upload/remove/set-main photo, `GET by barcode`,
  OFF lookup proxy (cached, rate-limited). Auth on every endpoint.
- Acceptance: unit + integration tests; review tries access without token /
  with another household's ids; DB size/mtime check.
- Model: Opus implementer + Opus review (migration, data, storage).

### Stage 3 — Product card UI: add photo and barcode
- Product card: photo gallery (take / pick, set main, delete), barcodes list,
  «Сканировать штрихкод» to add one.
- Purchase line sheet: «Сфотографировать товар» / «Сканировать» attaches to
  the linked product (or links the line first).
- Product list and search show the main photo thumbnail.
- Model: Sonnet implementer, Opus review (camera, big UI).

### Stage 4 — «Сканировать» in the shop
- Button on Главная (and products tab): camera → barcode → product card summary
  sheet: last purchase, price history, cheapest store, rating, «не брать» mark.
- Not found: search own products to link, or create from OFF prefill
  (name/brand/photo), or "remember for later".
- Optional «больше не покупать» flag on a product (shown red in scan result).
- Model: Sonnet implementer, Opus review.

### Stage 5 — Find by photo without a barcode
- Take a photo → backend sends it with candidate products (names + main photo
  thumbnails, pre-filtered by category/name) to a vision model → top 3 with
  confidence; person confirms.
- Measure first on a labelled set (owner's product photos, ≥30 items): top-1 /
  top-3 hit rate, time, cost per search. Alternatives to compare: image
  embeddings vs vision LLM. Announced budget before any paid call (est. under
  $0.01 per search, test set under $1).
- Model: Opus (recognition stage; numbers before/after required).

### Stage 6 — One photo of the whole haul → receipt lines
- After saving a receipt: «Сфотографировать покупки» → one photo of items on
  the table → model finds items, crops each, proposes a receipt line for each.
- Person confirms with the existing swipe review; confirmed crops become
  product photos.
- Measure on labelled hauls (≥5 photos with known receipts): correct pairs,
  wrong pairs, time, cost. Budget announced first.
- Model: Opus.

## Not now
- Video recognition (heavy for phone and Render free; little gain over photos).
- Product variants / package editions UI.
- Offline scan (needs local product cache; consider after stage 4 if cold start
  remains a problem).

## Order and dependencies
1 → 2 → 3 → 4 are the core (barcode works without AI). 5 and 6 depend on
products having photos, so they come after some weeks of real use.
