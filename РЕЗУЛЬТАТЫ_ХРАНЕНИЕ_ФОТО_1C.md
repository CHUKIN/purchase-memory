# Results: stage 1c, where product photos and barcodes live (decision prep)

Date: 2026-10-16. Read-only measurement of prod Supabase (`execute_sql` SELECTs
on `storage.objects` and counts in `purchase_memory`); no changes. Plan:
`ПЛАН_ФОТО_И_ШТРИХКОД_ТОВАРА.md` § Stage 1c. Owner decides.

## Measured today (prod)
- Storage bucket `receipts`: 18 files, 38 MB (3.8% of the free 1 GB).
- 9 receipt captures → per receipt: original ~3.4–3.8 MB + display copy
  ~0.45–0.76 MB ≈ 4.3 MB. Originals are ~85% of all bytes.
- Database 12 MB total, app schema 1.6 MB (50 products, 79 items) — far from
  the 500 MB DB limit.

| Usage | Per month | 1 GB full in |

Barcodes, flags, photo metadata: tiny rows → existing Postgres, no new service.

## Options for photo files (prices checked 2026-10-16)
1. **Supabase Storage (current).** No new account, code exists
   (`IReceiptBlobStorage`, S3 API). Free 1 GB storage, 5 GB egress/month;
   project pauses after 1 week idle (data kept, app down until restored). Pro
   $25/month (100 GB).
2. **Cloudflare R2.** Free 10 GB-month, 1M writes, 10M reads per month, no
   egress fees; then $0.015/GB-month. Same S3 client code; EU jurisdiction
   option. Needs the owner's personal Cloudflare account + key in `~/.zshrc`
   and on Render.
3. Backblaze B2 (free 10 GB) — similar to R2, no advantage here.
4. Render disk — needs a paid instance; no.

## Recommendation
- Keep Supabase Storage now; put product photos there behind the same storage
  contract (config-only switch later).
- Main risk is receipt originals, not product photos. Separate small stage:
  after recognition, keep only the display copy (or a ~1 MB re-compressed
  original). Needs a check that line boxes/crops and re-recognition still work
  (boxes are drawn on the display copy already, see photo rotation note in
  `ЧТО_ДАЛЬШЕ.md`).
- Move to R2 if usage passes ~70% of 1 GB, or together with the
  infrastructure decision (Supabase pause / backups, `ЧТО_ДАЛЬШЕ.md` §3).
- Thumbnails: generate on upload (~20 KB) for lists and scan result.
- Backups of the bucket: part of the infrastructure decision.

## Owner decision (2026-10-16)
- Keep Supabase Storage for product photos.
- Store receipts shrunk after recognition: added to `ЧТО_ДАЛЬШЕ.md` as a
  future candidate stage (not started).
