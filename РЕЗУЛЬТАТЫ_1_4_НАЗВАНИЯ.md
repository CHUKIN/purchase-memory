# Stage 1.4 — Human product names (PL + RU): results

Level: Полный (migration + AI output). Branch `feature/product-names` (from main f53c402), uncommitted. Design: Variant 1 (Polish on top, Russian under it).

## Done

- **Domain + migration `AddProductDisplayNames`** (SQLite `20261029214537`, Postgres `20261029214546`).
  - `products` gets `NamePl`, `NormalizedNamePl`, `NamePlSource`, `NameRu`, `NormalizedNameRu`, `NameRuSource` and `NamingAttemptedAt`. All are nullable, nothing is rewritten, and Down drops them.
  - `CanonicalName` (the receipt text) is unchanged.
  - Sources follow the category/brand pattern: `Suggested` / `Confirmed` / null. The AI never writes over `Confirmed`; this is guarded by conditional `ExecuteUpdate` statements.
  - A confirmed empty name means "show the receipt text"; the AI leaves it alone.
  - A merge takes over the other product's confirmed names (each language on its own).
- **Generation**: the same categorizer request (`gpt-6-luna`, reasoning Low, batches of 40, unchanged settings) now also returns `namePl` / `nameRu`.
  - The schema is strict; v2 adds the two nullable fields. A known brand is passed as a hint (`[brand: …]`).
  - After a save, products with missing names are picked up together with unclassified ones.
  - The backfill (`POST /api/products/categorization/backfill`) also fills names that are missing (source null). Suggested names stay. Products that were picked only for their names keep their category and brand.
  - `NamingAttemptedAt` stops the backfill from paying again for a product whose name came back null. Pass `retryAttempted=true` to ask again.
- **API**
  - `PUT /api/products/{id}/name` takes `{ name, language: "pl"|"ru" }` and sets that name as confirmed (empty clears it). Without `language` it is the old rename of the receipt text.
  - `POST /api/products/{id}/names/confirm` is «Верно»: it confirms every suggested name.
  - `POST /api/products/{id}/name-suggestions` is «Предложить заново»: a paid call with its own per-user bucket `name-suggestions` (30 burst, then 1 per 2 min).
    - It replaces suggested names only. With both names confirmed it makes no call.
    - It returns 503 `nameSuggestionsUnavailable` when there is no model or the call fails.
  - Product list, detail and change DTOs carry `namePl`, `namePlSource`, `nameRu` and `nameRuSource`. Purchase lines carry `productNamePl`. The backfill summary has `namesSuggested`. The MCP product search returns the names.
  - Access: approved-user fallback policy, same as every product endpoint. There are no households in this version.
- **Search**: the catalog `q` also matches `NormalizedNamePl` / `NormalizedNameRu`, the stored `ProductNameNormalizer` keys. Cyrillic is kept, and the search does not depend on the database collation. The catalog is sorted by the Polish name, else the receipt text.
- **UI (Variant 1)**
  - Lists, the search modal and scan results: the Polish name is large (with ✦ while suggested). The Russian name comes first in the grey line, then category · brand · purchases.
  - Product card: the Polish title, then the Russian name and «В чеке: <raw>».
    - The «Названия» block has both names with «предложено», a pencil to edit each (IonAlert), «Верно» and «Предложить заново».
    - A missing name falls back to the receipt text, with no mark.
  - Purchase lines keep the receipt text as the name, with «→ Kefir naturalny …» under it.
- **Review hub**: the section «Названия предложено» sits between Товары and Категории. The kind is `suggestedNames` (hub only, never for flagged products), and its count is in `summary` (total = sum of sections). It has «Верно» / «Изменить», and card mode with «Верно, дальше». Category items show the Polish name.
- **Help**: new topic `product-names` (ru + en). The `products` and `review-queue` topics are updated, and button names are checked against `i18n.ts`. The catalog search placeholder now reads «Название или текст из чека».
- **Stories**: Card (`WithNames*`, `NamesConfirmed`, `NamesMissing`), Catalog `WithHumanNamesMobileRu`, and Screens (a kefir line in the purchase detail, names in the hub, `ReviewNamesCardsMobile`).
